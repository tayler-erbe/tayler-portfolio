09
vLLM vs Ollama Inference Throughput Evaluation

Performance engineering case study on a single NVIDIA L4 GPU. Controlled benchmark methodology isolated request serialization in the production Ollama serving layer as the throughput bottleneck — not the hardware or model. Migration to vLLM continuous batching delivered a 6.22× speedup at C=8 on the same hardware and same model class, taking the 12,125-image archival corpus from 22 hours to 3.7 hours with GPU utilization rising from 28% to 97%.

10
vLLM Multi-Model Sweep · Legislation Pipeline

Controlled 54-cell benchmark across three open-weight 7-8B AWQ models (Mistral, Qwen 2.5, Llama 3.1) on a single NVIDIA L4 to defend or replace the production model choice for the Legislative Intelligence Platform. Production concurrency=4, six chunk sizes, three repeats, full per-request telemetry (TTFT, ITL p99, decode/prefill throughput) and server-side GPU sampling. 8h 34m unattended runtime, 0 alarms fired, 4,586 per-chunk samples. Identified Llama 3.1 8B AWQ as the production winner with 89.5-92.5% parse OK across all 15 extraction fields versus 56-72% on the incumbent Mistral. Methodology ports to any future GPU upgrade as the comparison baseline.
